## D-020 — A name is an identity only within an office
Date: 2026-09-06
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: In payroll sources a staffer name is scoped to the member office above it (Statement of Disbursements structure: member, then staff rows). Cross-office identity must be evidenced; the discriminator is day-level service dates, not quarter overlap. Same-quarter multi-office names carrying the shared-employee title are one person; day-level overlapping service in two offices without it is a true collision and must be split.
Context: Paul, 2026-09-02: "Split the same-name collisions apart... the staffer names always appear directly below the members." Measured on the House ledger v1: SHARED_EMPLOYEE 7,605, SEQUENTIAL_ONLY 3,934, MID_QUARTER_MOVE 161, TRUE_COLLISION 23 of 60,136.
Rationale: Global name matching merges strangers; office scoping plus dates is what the documents actually assert.
Reversal condition: A licensed corpus with person identifiers (LegiStorm, D-Q2) replaces name scoping.
