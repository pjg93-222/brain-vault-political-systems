## D-006 — Sessions commit to the brain directly
Date: 2026-08-13
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Sessions may write into this brain and commit it directly; the draft-and-hand-to-Paul rule is retired.
Context: The brain was built on the principle that sessions draft and only Paul runs brainctl. After the first real seeding Paul granted the override standing, so future sessions do not have to ask per-commit.
Rationale: The per-commit gate was costing a round trip on every durable fact without adding safety - entries are append-only, reversible by supersession, and visible in git, so a wrong entry is correctable rather than destructive.
Reversal condition: Paul withdraws the override, or a session writes an entry that is inaccurate or unsourced and the error survives into a later session's reasoning.
