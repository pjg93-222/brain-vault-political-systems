## D-061 - The split-view strip is retired; the Board is how Chart Studio shows several charts
Date: 2026-09-19
Status: ACTIVE - `2b1a62dea` on codex/mentat-crypto.
Decision: Chart Studio has one way to show several charts: the Board (Canvas mode, 1, 2 or 4 sheets of one workspace, [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-041|D-041]]'s clean-up lane, `3eeb99e00`). The 2026-09-13 split view - the "1 chart | 2 charts | 3 charts | 4 charts | Canvas only in every chart | Linked filters" strip whose extra charts were same-origin iframes (?studioPane=B|C|D) each running a whole studio - is removed, and with it linked filters across panes, independent builders side by side and the three-chart layout. `studioPane` is a retired URL key: ignored, preserved, never an error, so an old link opens the ordinary studio. With no pane iframes left, every response carries Content-Security-Policy frame-ancestors 'none'.
Context: Paul's ruling, verbatim: "Remove the N charts strip, keep the Board", after the reconcile lane flagged that two controls answered the same question and asked rather than removing it unasked.
Rationale: His brief for the clean-up was ease of use, conciseness, symmetry and de-cluttering. The strip cost 60 px above every chart and six controls; a workspace already holds sheets from different industries, so the Board covers side-by-side comparison. Removing the only iframe user lets the frame policy go back to 'none'.
Reversal condition: Paul wants linked filters or independent side-by-side builders back. `git revert 2b1a62dea` restores the strip whole; the stored pane data was never deleted.
