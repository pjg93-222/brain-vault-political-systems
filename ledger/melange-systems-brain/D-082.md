## D-082 — Near ties in the Staffer work_time search go to the route with fewer steps
Date: 2026-10-03
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: In the Staffer (work_time) route search, routes whose exact costs lie within 1e-12 relative of the cheapest are ties; the fewest steps wins, then the lower exact cost, then the smallest node ids read from the destination end. Exact cost is the real sum of the float hop and terminal costs; the reported cost stays the forward float sum. Ranked alternatives follow the same rule among pending candidates; the window is judged within each alternative's own spur search, so two alternatives that tie only over the whole route can occasionally appear out of step order (documented in the code and README).
Context: Paul 2026-10-03, "b on ties". The bidirectional search adds forward and backward sums in a different order from the old one-way search; in 40,000 tie-heavy synthetic graphs that changed the step count in 102 cases. Implemented as a float pass with an extended stop plus an exact pass over the near-tie routes: 0 rule violations in 40,000 tie-heavy graphs and exact agreement with full enumeration on 6,000 small graphs.
Rationale: The documented rule is "equal cost goes to fewer steps"; the old choice in float near-ties was a rounding accident.
Reversal condition: Paul rules another tolerance, or a case shows the window hiding a route that differs in a real quantity.
