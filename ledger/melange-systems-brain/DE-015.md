## DE-015 - Running the CU negative screen over a discovery record's headline text
Date: 2026-09-06
Approach: Applying [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/DE-001|DE-001]]'s sanctioned negative screen (zero CU terms implies no CU provisions) to a GovInfo discovery record's own fields - title, heading and raw_text, 144-484 chars - rather than to the act's operative text. Used to triage the 22 congress_advanced_current discovery candidates.
Failure mode: Five false negatives. The headline screen found CU terms in 2 of 22 candidates; the same screen over the official GovInfo BILLS/BILLSTATUS XML found 7. Missed 119:hr:10184, 119:hr:10242, 119:s:5335, 119:s:5358 and 119:sres:817 - including 119:s:5335, the strongest shared candidate in the set (it amends the FFIEC Act of 1978, and NCUA is a statutory FFIEC member).
Root cause: congress_advanced_records.ndjson raw_text is the concatenated Congress.gov search-result row - display number, title, sponsor line, latest action - and not bill text. A screen whose evidential force comes from exhaustiveness over the operative text cannot be run over a headline; absence of a term there is absence from the summary, not absence from the act.
Would reconsider if: never for the headline substrate. The negative screen is valid only over official GovInfo BILLS/BILLSTATUS XML. Note the positive direction is unaffected - a CU term found in a headline is still a true positive; it is only the ZERO reading that is invalid.
