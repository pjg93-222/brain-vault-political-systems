## D-028 - Primacy board and Leg% use a purity-anchored delivery mix
Date: 2026-09-12
Status: ACTIVE - implementation pending (Codex quota returns 2026-09-17)

The original rankings were better before") and, after a seven-variant comparison and a purity-floor sweep, ruled "0.80, and switch Leg% to the same blend". Facts at ruling: today's Tier 1 is exactly the 13 gated Champions; at f=0.80 the mix keeps 10 of them, promotes Kim, Huizenga and Daines from Tier 2 ranks 14-16, moves Crapo, Vargas and Rounds to Tier 2, changes 74 of 533 tiers, agrees with today's board at Spearman 0.83, and holds mid-purity high-volume members (min Tier-1 purity 0.556) out of the top tiers; additive blends fail that test at every weight below 0.9. Rationale: The board exists to name champions, so purity must remain the ranking key (the 2026-07-29 "purity ranks" intent) while delivery reorders within the champion band; a product enforces a purity bar that a weighted sum cannot. One delivery definition for the board and the rank keeps the two surfaces from contradicting each other.
Reversal condition: Paul reverses after seeing the activated board and ranks; or SS calibration lands and the substance term needs re-weighting (that is a new entry, not a silent edit of the 0.35/0.35/0.30 split).
