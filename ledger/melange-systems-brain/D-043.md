## D-043 - A row's own text can contradict its company attribution
Date: 2026-09-18
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Amends [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-040|D-040]], which settled that ambiguity counts. This settles what counts as a contradiction on the attribution axis: the row's own text decides. A cross-verification row whose employer (individual) or contributor name (entity) states a different identifiable organisation, judged by whole-token matching against the company's roster variants and inputs/verified_employer_aliases.csv, is CONTRADICTED and is excluded under reason `attribution_contradicted_by_text`. Text that names the listed company or one of its verified aliases keeps the row counted and flagged under `company_named_unverified`. Blank text, "not employed", "none" and other non-naming text keeps the row counted and flagged as before. A donor employed elsewhere is still supported by either of two routes, both of which change verification status only and never decide dollars: a RECIPIENT route, where the recipient committee is the company's dated PAC or the FEC committee master's connected-organisation field resolves to the company, and a ROLE route for advisor, adviser, advisory, consultant, contractor, trustee, partner, counsel and board markers where the text also names the company. Plain "director" is a bank job rank and is not a marker; "retired" is a former employee and is not a marker, though those rows count anyway because their text names the company. No row is ever attributed to a DIFFERENT listed company by this test.
