## D-023 — Organisation membership for LDA filers: most recent employer; contract filers tied to every client
Date: 2026-09-07
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: A lobbyist (filer) is listed as an employee of one organisation only — the registrant on the chronologically most recent filing that names them (tie: the registrant with more filings in that period); earlier registrants are kept as former employers in the person's profile, not as memberships. A contract filer — one whose most recent registrant files on behalf of clients other than itself — is additionally tied to every client organisation on filings that name them, listed under each client as "represented by". This is the ruling, not duplication. The employer line reads "Employer on file: <most recent registrant>" with an as-of period and, for contract filers, a client count.
Context: Paul, 2026-09-07, verbatim: "For the filer, you do what is chronologically the most recent. If it appears they are contract/mercenary they are tied to all those organizations as clients." Static organisations payload and the 8841 Groups index both implement it; the LDA corpus supplies registrant, client and period per filing.
Rationale: One employer per person keeps the surface free of duplication; the client ties are the real structure of contract lobbying and are what a reader of an organisation wants to see.
Reversal condition: Paul withdraws it explicitly.
