## D-162 — Sentinel source-outage rule: carry admitted verdicts up to 30 days, never new ones
Date: 2026-10-11
Status: ACTIVE — decided by Claude under Paul's delegation 2026-10-11. Melange commit: see git log --grep "[[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-162|D-162]]".
Decision: When the House Code service returns its maintenance page or a 5xx, refresh_sentinel_admissions.py raises SourceUnavailable (not StatuteIdentityMismatch). An ADMITTED bill keeps its published include if its official govinfo bill text is unchanged, its cached evidence re-hashes, it re-validates, and its checked_at (last real verification, kept unchanged) is at most 30 days old (SA.CARRY_FORWARD_MAX_DAYS); the entry records carried_forward: source_unavailable, carried_at and last_verified_at, and SA.load enforces the 30-day cap. Otherwise the refresh fails as before. A NEW measure is never carried or held: it is deferred untouched (source_unavailable_deferred_keys). govinfo 5xx responses are retried within the existing 3-try bound. No pre-fetch reuse was added: reuse must compare fresh evidence, so statute changes still invalidate a verdict.
Context: Decided by Claude under Paul's delegation 2026-10-11 (option b, not another widening of [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-122|D-122]]). The 2026-10-11 weekly refresh failed: uscode.house.gov served its 'Under Maintenance' page for 12 admitted bills (read as identity mismatch) and HTTP 500 for 8 new candidates (the orchestrator's 'govinfo 500s' were actually House Code 500s, reproduced by hand); refresh_sentinel_admissions.py installs nothing on any error, so sentinel_admissions.json (checked 2026-10-01) aged past [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-122|D-122]]'s 10-day window.
Rationale: An outage of the statute service says nothing about a bill. Carrying a verified include on hash-checked cached evidence and unchanged official bill text is safer than widening the freshness window for every bill, and the 30-day cap plus the consumer check in sentinel_admissions.load bound the staleness. checked_at is never forged ([[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-122|D-122]]).
Reversal condition: Paul rules otherwise; or a carried verdict is later found wrong because a cited statute changed during an outage.
