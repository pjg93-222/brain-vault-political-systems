## DE-076 — Launching a chain attempt without checking the chain transac
Date: 2026-10-21
Approach: Launching a chain attempt without checking the chain transaction's 30 GB disk floor at launch: attempt 6 passed every congress step, then the pipeline step refused to start (before-image 1.38 GB would leave 12.95 GB free; free then 14.32 GB).
Failure mode: preconditions.ps1 was run with -MinFreeGB 20 (the handoff and plan gate), so the launch cleared a gate lower than the one the chain enforces, and the wrapper had no launch-time check. About 35 minutes of discovery and admissions work was done and rolled back; no chain step ran and no ranks moved.
Root cause: Two independent disk floors (E2E preconditions 20 GB, chain transaction 30 GB), and a C: drive that fell from 49.5 GB to 13 GB in 3 hours: an 18.3 GB non-sparse worktree made by the deployment-readiness session, 10.5 GB of Codex Terrier test-set zips and about 3 GB of speech-lane output. About 22 GB came back when the deployment session removed its worktree and a build folder.

Relaunch preconditions use -MinFreeGB 32 and a manual free-disk check.
