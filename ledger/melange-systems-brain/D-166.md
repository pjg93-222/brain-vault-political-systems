## D-166 — Front door: Cloudflare Tunnel + Access
Date: 2026-10-11
Status: ACTIVE — decided by Paul 2026-10-11 (ruling 3; plan approval).
Decision: Zero inbound ports: the VPS is reached only through a Cloudflare Tunnel. One Cloudflare Access app per host (melange, mentat, co05, or05, ct01, ssh); groups paul-admin, staff, collaborators, plus terrier-co05, terrier-or05 and terrier-ct01 (Terrier is US-only); MFA; service tokens svc-push and svc-status. Every app also verifies the Access JWT itself (RS256, aud, iss, exp, email) and fails closed. Cloudflare is recorded as a data processor (it sees plaintext).
Context: Plan Phase 2 adds one stdlib access_gate.py to fec/, mentat/app/ and Terrier server/research/; Terrier's AccessJwtMiddleware also requires the JWT email to equal the logged-in session user. Phase 0 (Paul) moves a domain's nameservers to Cloudflare and sets every tunnel route to Protect with Access. Free-plan Access logs are kept 24 h, so the plan adds an hourly log export and alerts. Source: plan C:\Users\pgoff\.claude\plans\we-are-going-to-dapper-sunrise.md, approved by Paul 2026-10-11; Paul's rulings were relayed in the orchestrator's brief for this brain update, so no verbatim quote is available to this lane (same as [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-140|D-140]]).
Rationale: Access gives MFA, groups and logs without opening a port, and the per-app JWT check means an Access misconfiguration or a stray tunnel connector does not by itself expose an app. Cloudflare proxies plaintext, mitigated by cache bypass and no-store headers and by recording it as a processor.
Reversal condition: Cloudflare is unacceptable as a data processor, or Free-plan limits bind.
