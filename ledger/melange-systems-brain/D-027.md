## D-027 - The realized-results board is chamber-normalized
Date: 2026-09-12
Status: ACTIVE - implementation pending (Codex quota returns 2026-09-17)
Decision: The realized_results primacy board ranks members on the within-chamber percentile of signed realized results among gate-qualified members, not on the raw cross-chamber value; ties break on the raw value, then name. Seats stay 13/24/48; the distinct-measure gate (minimum 1) and the conduct ladder are unchanged by this ruling.
Context: The shadow board (realized_results_v1) ranked raw values across chambers; 118th House values run to 0.558 and Senate to 0.130, so Tier 1 and Tier 2 were 100 percent House and all six legacy Senate Tier-1 members fell to ranks 90-231. Paul, 2026-09-12: "chamber-normalize the board".
Rationale: The paper's score is defined per chamber-term and only compares within a chamber; a cross-chamber raw sort turns chamber size and bill volume into rank. The reporting rank already uses within-chamber percentiles; the board must not contradict it.
Reversal condition: Paul ratifies a different comparability rule (for example fixed per-chamber seat quotas) after seeing the normalized board, or evidence that within-chamber percentiles hide a real cross-chamber difference the board is meant to show.
