## D-030 - Industry is a request parameter, never a console global
Date: 2026-09-16
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: One Mentat console addresses every published snapshot. Every industry-scoped route takes `industry` on the request (the published label; its `contribution_data._slug` underscore form also accepted; the label always echoed with the snapshot's `build_stamp`); `dataset` stays a legacy alias carrying the same value; the resolution rule is industry, else dataset, disagreement 400, neither = the route's legacy behaviour; no process-level industry setting exists or may be added; saved sheets and their series sources persist their industry; the snapshot registry (`GET /api/snapshots`) is the single list of what one console can address and never publishes an absolute path.
Context: S4 of the mentat-failings lane, design accepted by Paul 2026-09-16 ("The condition is met, proceed") after measurement showed the server was already stateless on industry and the dataset id was the industry label; committed `8377b5e77`, `9262614c2`, `b13d09018`.
Rationale: a console global would force one console per industry and make a two-industry chart impossible; the parameter form costs 73 ms per request warm and lets S5 mix industries per series while every existing caller keeps working.
Reversal condition: a snapshot set too large for one process, or a measured per-request registry cost the console cannot afford after memoization is tried.
