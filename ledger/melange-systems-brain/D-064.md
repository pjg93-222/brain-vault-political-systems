## D-064 — The Giving Forecast applies to Mentat's own data; OpenSecrets only estimates the relationships
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Projections carry the models' % change (PAC, lobbying) or percentage-point change (partisan share) onto Mentat's own previous-cycle values; levels always come from Mentat. PAC is shown twice, side by side: the roster companies' PACs' own giving to candidates (read-only from the FEC pas2 files Mentat already holds, under the [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-049|D-049]] filing-version rule) and Mentat general giving. Partisan share is shown on both bases (employee candidate giving; PAC candidate giving), with the 2024->2026 control input Polymarket-to-Polymarket, January-June. Lobbying levels come from Melange LDA, both series (outside-firm fees and in-house expenses) side by side and never summed; the lobbying slope is a log refit on LobbyView. Auto is not displayed (no Mentat data); Community Banks stays separate.
Context: Paul's ruling, 2026-09-30, in the forecast lane (worktree forecasting-model-audit-5603f2).
Rationale: OpenSecrets data only estimates the relationships; Mentat's own data is the source of every level shown.
Reversal condition: Mentat holds enough cycles (six or more) to fit the models on its own data.
