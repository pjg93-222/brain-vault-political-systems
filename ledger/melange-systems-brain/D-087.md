## D-087 — Staffer co-worker rows are not pre-scored in bulk
Date: 2026-10-03
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: The cold-path speed round is limited to three changes: cached per-search lookups, background prewarm of the work and network indexes after each router publish, and skipping zero-overlap pairs before scoring. Rows are not pre-scored in bulk, and they are not kept across the daily as_of change.
Context: Paul 2026-10-03, choosing options 1-3 of 5. Full pre-scoring would make every search run at warm speed (first route 0.1-3 s), but it costs +0.15-0.3 GB of sidecar RAM and 3-12 min of CPU per rebuild; host free RAM fell to 0.65 GB during the 2026-10-03 panel.
Rationale: Memory headroom on this host is the binding constraint.
Reversal condition: More RAM headroom, or Paul asks for warm-speed first searches.
