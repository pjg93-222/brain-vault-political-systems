## D-421 — Briefs v3 review bar: block only unsupported claims and contradictions; keep it simple (Paul 2026-10-29)
Date: 2026-10-29
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: A rewritten brief passes review when it is a narrative built on the packet evidence with no unsupported claims (no claim beyond the evidence, no fact stated stronger or weaker than its band, no citation outside may_support) and no contradictions (including a reason that cuts against its own advice).
