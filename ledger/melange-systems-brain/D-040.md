## D-040 - The Mentat cross-verification excludes only on contradiction, never on ambiguity; its console tab publishes no totals
Date: 2026-09-17
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: (a) A cross-verification step (the Python engine in mentat/tools/cross_reference_engine.py and the R receipt-evidence gate fec_assert_receipt_evidence in mentat/R/collect.R) may remove or zero a row only when the evidence CONTRADICTS it: a proven duplicate of a counted parent, a proven refund link, a superseded filing version, a non-giving receipt role stated by the filer, or a company match the collector REJECTED. Missing or ambiguous information (a prefix match under review, a memo leg whose parent is not in the corpus, a blank description on an other-receipts line, a blank line number) never excludes: the row counts and carries an unverified flag (attribution_status unverified / receipt_role_unverified). The export gate therefore refuses a build only for contradictions. (b) The console's Cross-reference tab is dwarfed: it lists clear refunds and obvious outliers only, publishes no total, and the published <Industry> Industry Spending Overview.xlsx is the accounting of record. Outliers are relative to the run's own distribution per entity class (percentile and median multiple), not absolute dollar thresholds. (c) "Crypto's money is correct": the Coinbase receipts to FAIRSHAKE and CLF reported with a blank line number and blank description ($19,767,475) are giving and count.
Context: Paul's rulings of 2026-09-17: in the S3 session at 11:29 ET ("way too strict ... we are focusing on contradictions, not ambiguity ... if you can improve the process to reduce this, then do it"); in the cross-reference/workbook lane ("it itself is redundant and cannot capture the nuance of political giving, It can be maintained but at a dwarfed state only for obvious outliers or clear refunds", then "'contradictions, not ambiguity' engine rework. This is the one", "Crypto's money is correct", "Outliers should be relative"). Measured basis in audits/mentat_xref_workbook_review_20260917/ in the Mentat tree: the Banking run zeroed 36,241 rows / $37,940,955.71 for an unverified prefix match that R holds in review; the export gate refused 948 rows / $192.0M across the eight industries, 503 of them with no description at all.
Rationale: three unreconciled totals on one console was the user harm, and both exclusion engines were dropping money on absence of evidence rather than on evidence against it. The name, amount and date filters that admitted a row are the intended defence against noise; a flag keeps the ambiguity visible without zeroing it.
Reversal condition: Paul asks for a reconciled total on the tab again, or a measured false-positive rate among counted-with-flag rows that he judges unacceptable.
