## DE-020 - Treating a budgeted collector's designed PARTIAL exit as a fatal daily failure
Date: 2026-09-12/13
Approach: The 2026-09-12 daily-refresh wrapper design treated the press collector's designed PARTIAL exit (refresh_press_delta.R exit 3 = body backlog remains) as a fatal daily failure rather than an expected, budgeted outcome.
Failure mode: With a 2,200-URL pending-body backlog and a 600 s per-member budget, the day can never complete in one pass, so the hourly retry task re-ran the full 4-hour chain thirteen times on 2026-09-13, re-spending 24 Claude CLI calls per pass.
Root cause: The wrapper had no PARTIAL state distinct from FAIL, so a by-design budget shortfall (JS_MEMBER_BUDGET below the backlog, fold OFF) was indistinguishable from a real failure and triggered the same unconditional hourly retry.
Would reconsider if: the backlog is drained (budget raised or fold enabled) AND the intake gate build_press_engine_feed.py:99-101 accepts the generation; until then, PARTIAL parks the intake steps and closes the day with parked_steps visible in the receipt.
