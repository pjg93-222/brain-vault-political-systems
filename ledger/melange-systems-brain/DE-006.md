## DE-006 - Member-level hidden-Markov / latent-state layer over the existing channels
Date: 2026-08-19
Approach: Adding a hidden Markov model (or any per-member discrete latent-state sequence layer) whose states are a member's unobserved stance/mobilization regime, whose emissions are the member's press/tweet/cosponsorship activity, and whose transitions are driven by adjacent colleagues' media statements plus trade-association activity as an exogenous priming signal. Four formulations were designed independently and each adversarially verified: (A) per-member stance regime, (B) network-coupled IOHMM, (C) issue-level regime HSMM with duration, (D) an is-HMM-even-right challenger.
Failure mode: Four independent designs failed the same way - the fitted states resolve to a VOLUME PARTITION ("cosponsors a lot" vs "cosponsors a little"), and three of the four canonicalized label-switching by ordering states on the emission mean, which labels the states by volume rank by construction. Three further measured blockers, each independently sufficient: (1) the core premise is ALREADY MEASURED NULL - fec/stance/stance_dynamics.py tests neighbour co-movement on the cosponsor graph ("an empirical validation of the cascade premise itself", its docstring line 15) and data/stance/stance_dynamics.json records same 134 / opposite 122 = 52.3% against the function's own 55% dominance threshold, so stance_dynamics.py:106 prints "null: no homophily in temporal movement on this layer" (n=256 mover-neighbour pairs, 18 windows of 91 days, 373 members tracked); (2) the statement channel fails the density bar already pinned by [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/DE-002|DE-002]]'s kill in the Melange repo (fec_lda_round4_family_prereg.md:48-52, "median unit >= 6 nonzero periods") - measured at 0.68 non-static transitions per member across 17 window-pairs, with 242/533 members holding zero press documents and stance_dynamics.py:56 stating "our members publish quarterly at best"; (3) the exogenous trade-association channel has no variance to prime with - the three CU associations file under five LDA issue codes total, 76.8% on BAN alone and BAN+FIN 96.5%, while the sector series is monotone drift (orgs 19->25, filings 30-38 across 22 quarters).
Root cause: The project's observable member behaviour is dominated by activity VOLUME, and volume is already the thing every reliable-looking measure here collapses to - cooperation_z was 2/3 raw cosponsorship volume (big5_a_matrix_prereg.md:6) and broad text-N was face-invalid. A latent state estimated from these channels has nothing left to be once volume is removed. Secondarily, LD-2 is a deadline-batched LAGGING disclosure (~95% of rows landing in four months, 20-110 day lag), so it cannot prime the quarter it describes.
Would reconsider if: ALL FOUR hold. (a) Per-post category assignment exists for the ~9,465 Quorum rows - today the social channel carries zero category labels (four of six tested category names return no hits), so no issue-indexed state model is possible at any K. (b) A markup / hearing-notice collection campaign lands a genuinely leading, member-free, densely-dated channel - current density is 77 distinct hearing dates across 2019-2026, about 0.2 per issue-quarter. (c) Per-org quarterly LD-1 registration and termination event series are built (derivable from the sweeps on disk, not shipped) - these are the only genuinely LEADING LDA events, and [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/DE-002|DE-002]]'s own successor condition names trade-association-inclusive attention as the density fix. (d) Paul rules on the scoring-authority question: all 218,875 rows of member_measure_edges.ndjson carry "scoring_authority": false / "evidence_scope": "review_prefill_only", so promoting that file to the outcome of a preregistered forecast is a change-control event. Even with all four, the askable question is a SECTOR-level K=2 state ("are association notices elevated while member arrivals are not?"), never a member-level K=3 - 299 CU-association bills over 22 quarters will not divide 18 ways.
Note on scope: what is dead is the member-level latent-state layer, not sequence modelling as such. The cosponsorship event stream is real and dense (204,021 dated sign-ons, congresses 116-119, median 153.5 per member over 117-119) and remains the substrate for the non-latent successors recorded alongside this entry.
