## D-083 — Search never matches fragments of machine identifiers
Date: 2026-10-03
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Staffer typeahead (profile and people search), the map filter and the record browser match pieces of a query only against human-readable text: names, labels, titles, aliases, summaries, organisation and office names, and the name part of committee ids. Machine identifiers (node ids, politico:<uuid>, bare uuids, bioguide and policy ids, the structural prefix of committee ids, hashes) match only when the whole query equals the identifier, in any case, and the matching person or rows are listed first. A whole bioguide id lists that member's staff.
Context: Paul 2026-10-03, "fix the uuid match". Profile search matched substrings of politico:<uuid>, so "co", "po" and "li" returned every profile, and bioguide pieces such as "b0" returned 60 unrelated staffers. The follow-up extended the rule to the map filter and record browser; it first treated whole committee ids as opaque, and the committee-name part was made searchable again in the next landing, which Paul approved.
Rationale: Identifier fragments produce junk matches and slow short queries; whole identifiers stay usable for pasting.
Reversal condition: Paul wants identifier-fragment search back on a surface.
Outcome: typeahead LANDED 2026-10-03 (landing_v2); map filter and record browser LANDED 2026-10-03 (landing_v3_ids); committee-name restoration LANDED 2026-10-04 (landing_v4); the record browser stays first-committee-only (Paul 2026-10-03: "leave it first-committee only"): it searches each person's first member, committee and policy id, while the map filter searches all of them; all committed on LIVE main 3366a7840.
