## DE-016 - Re-running chain step 11 standalone after a completed chain
Date: 2026-09-06
Approach: Invoking `fec/legislation/verify_collector_roster_updates.py` by hand on a quiet box after a full `run_pipeline.py` chain had completed, as a determinism check - identical inputs expected to yield byte-identical output.
Failure mode: The accepted delta COLLAPSED from 202 rows to 3 (already-present 5,015 -> 5,214; queued 20 and unresolvable 910 unchanged), rewriting `collector_roster_updates.ndjson` from 147,969 B / 202 lines to 2,175 B / 3 lines and its receipt from 52,718 B to 17,031 B. Because `sync_sentinel_bill_ledger.py:411` had already pinned `current_delta.sha256` to the 202-row file, the standalone run left that receipt pinning a hash no longer on disk - a BROKEN BINDING with no step 12 behind it to rebind. Only a pre-taken backup restored it. The three new accepted rows were not even a subset of the 202 (they were B001287/H.R.2094, L000607/H.R.2094, S001212/H.R.987, all 119th cosponsor joins dated 2026-08-31), so the ledger act universe had moved too.
Root cause: NOT nondeterminism - the inputs genuinely differed. This producer consumes the PREVIOUS cycle's `member_desk_bundle.json`, and `fec/surfaces/make_member_desk.py:2034` (chain position `run_pipeline.py:226`) merges this producer's own output back INTO that bundle as its fourth membership source; the producer's own header states this at `run_pipeline.py:85-89`. The chain regenerated the bundle at 2026-09-06T20:58:47Z, 28 minutes after its step 11 ran. A standalone re-run therefore reads its own already-merged output and correctly reports those joins as already present. The 202 were absorbed, not lost.
Would reconsider if: never as a standalone post-chain invocation. The script is chain step 11 (`run_pipeline.py:95`) and is meaningful only in sequence with its consumers - `sync_sentinel_bill_ledger.py:411` (which owns the binding), `make_member_desk.py:2034`, and `audit/dashboard_integrity.py`. A genuine determinism test requires two back-to-back runs with NO intervening bundle rebuild; that test remains UNRUN. Note the general shape: a producer whose output is folded into its own next-cycle input is not idempotent across a chain boundary, and re-running it looks like a data loss when it is actually absorption.
