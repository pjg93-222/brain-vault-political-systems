## D-343 — Candidate party from the FEC candidate master per cycle, not the first filing
Date: 2026-10-26
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Paul ruling (standing rule): a candidate's party comes from the FEC candidate master file (cn<yy>/cn.txt, CAND_PTY_AFFILIATION) for the relevant cycle, never from the first filing. A candidate missing from that cycle's master falls back to the first filing's party and is flagged visibly (party_source=filing). Applies in every view that derives party (Flow, industry flow, Graph and any other).
Context: Follow-up to [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-342|D-342]] (cross-candidate filings stay as filed) and Mentat #39 (one CandidateNames for Flow and Graph labels); #39's report flagged that party still came from the first filed row. A before/after diff of money moving between parties is reported before merge; > $1M in any cycle or industry cell, or any change to money counting, stops for Paul (money-frozen rule).
Rationale: Paul's ruling 2026-10-26, verbatim: 'Yes: take candidate party from the official FEC candidate master file instead of the first filing; record it, add tests, and report which money moves between parties.' A mis-keyed filing (Coinbase PAC's ROSEN, DEM, under Deb Fischer's S2NE00094, REP) could otherwise set the ID's party from another candidate's row.
Reversal condition: Paul rules otherwise, or the FEC master is shown to be wrong for a candidate in a way the filings get right.
