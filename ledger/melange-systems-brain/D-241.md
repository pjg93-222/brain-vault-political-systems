## D-241 — Terrier is one app with no per-district access limits (Paul, 2026-10-18)
Date: 2026-10-18
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Terrier becomes one application: one server that serves every district (CO-05, OR-05, CT-01), one accounts database, one sign-in gate and one hostname; everyone with a Terrier account can work in all districts. This reverses Paul's 2026-10-07 ruling "one server process per district" (brain vault terrier/2026-10-06-terrier-rulings.md:107) and amends [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-165|D-165]]'s "one hosted app per district" clause and [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-166|D-166]]'s per-host Access applications and terrier-<district> groups. It rules [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-164|D-164]]'s draft default "voter rows the user's own district group only" (marked NOT yet ruled) as: no district limit.
Context: Decided by Paul, 2026-10-18. Paul asked: "Terrier needs to be one entire thing why are there different permissions and apps per district?" The survey found the per-district split was an implementation convenience (it avoided an accounts migration, TERRIER_MULTIDISTRICT_BLUEPRINT_2026-10-06.md:268) that [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-165|D-165]] and [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-166|D-166]] inherited; [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-165|D-165]]'s rationale gives no reason for it. Three options were offered (one app with district access; one surface with three backends; one app with no district limits) and Paul chose "One app, no district limits". Risk recorded: [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-164|D-164]]'s rationale cites state voter-list statutes and data terms (ORS 247.955; CGS 9-50d; Colorado data-provider terms). Whether those permit staff from one district to see another district's voter rows was not checked; flagged to Paul on 2026-10-18. Relates to [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-164|D-164]], [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-165|D-165]], [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-166|D-166]].
Rationale: One thing to run, update, back up and grant; the desktop client is already one Terrier app; access grants already allow several districts.
Reversal condition: A statute or data-licence term is found that forbids cross-district access to voter rows, or Paul re-scopes Terrier access.
