## D-042 — Committee relevance tiers: jurisdiction over credit unions, not bill importance
Date: 2026-09-18
Status: ACTIVE — applied directly to the LIVE workbook (not a git commit); verified cell-by-cell (`evidence/committee-retier-veterans.md`, `evidence/committee-score-recompute.md`).
Decision: The committee relevance marker on `Committee Data` tracks jurisdiction over credit unions as institutions, not the importance of the bills a committee handles. `[Highly]` 3.0 = can write credit-union law (Banking, Financial Services, Finance, Ways & Means, Small Business). `[Moderately]` 1.8 = writes law naming credit unions as a regulated party (Commerce; and, by this ruling, the Agriculture family). `[Slightly]` 0.9 = reaches credit unions as one affected party among many (Energy & Commerce, Judiciary, Oversight; and, by this ruling, Veterans' Affairs). Subcommittees take 1/6 of the parent weight; Chair/Ranking/Vice multiply the base by 3.0-7.5x by role and level; Committee score = (Membership + Leadership) / 2.
Applied 2026-09-18: Agriculture (full, 76 members), Agriculture - Commodity Markets, Digital Assets & Rural Development (22), Agriculture - Rural Development, Energy & Credit (13) = `[Moderately]`; Veterans' Affairs (full, 44 members, both chambers share one string) = `[Slightly]`, retiered from the `[Moderately]` placeholder [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-036|D-036]] shipped, with the 44 rows' Membership/Leadership/Committee score recomputed (92 cells; drops of 0.45 plain, 1.35 Ranking, 1.80 Chair; largest single drop Jerry Moran 5.7 -> 3.9; max committee score unchanged at 11.575, Mike Crapo).
Context: Paul, 2026-09-18, delegating the inference rather than ruling each body: "You should be able to infer from what committees are already ranked how to rank the rest, Finish the rest." The decisive evidence is Judiciary sitting at `[Slightly]` although it owns the Credit Card Competition Act, whose policy area carries the third-highest industry weight (Interchange, IMP 2.0) — so the marker cannot be a policy-weight proxy.
Rationale: Keeps the influence pillar a credit-union-relevance measure rather than a committee-count measure; the same reason the primacy board is purity-scored (2026-07-29).
Open item: `Committee score`/`Membership`/`Leadership` (columns B/C/D of `Committee Data`) are CACHED VALUES, not formulas, and no step of `run_pipeline.py` recomputes them — a future tier change must be followed by `recompute_committee_scores.py` or the seat text and the score disagree silently.
Preserved quirks (not part of this ruling, kept verbatim from the existing implementation): `apply_committee_rows.py` scores any seat named "Small Business" as `[Highly]` regardless of its displayed marker, and treats any name containing " - " as a subcommittee.
Reversal condition: Paul re-tiers in Excel (the markers are his to change, and the scores recompute with `audits/members_section_20260914/scripts/recompute_committee_scores.py`), or a Veterans'/Agriculture committee gains credit-union jurisdiction.
