The ruling names the Terrier store builds, the Mentat R/Python pipeline and the Melange chain plus collectors; it does not separately rule on the Edge-driven and claude-CLI steps inside them, so each lane design must say how those steps are handled. Excel recalculation is [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-174|D-174]]; where images are built and run is [[ledger/melange-systems-brain/D-175|D-175]].
