## D-065 — Forecast rules are chosen by out-of-sample accuracy, not by p-values
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: On every refit each term — and, for the partisan-share anchor, each industry and chamber — is backtested with no leakage in two designs (leave-one-election-out and chronological); a rule replaces the default (pooled slope; previous-cycle anchor) only if it wins by at least 5% in both designs. Rationale: Own PAC slopes cut error roughly in half under the previous-cycle anchor (leave-one-election-out 10.8 vs pooled 21.9, naive 12.7); tailoring by low in-sample p never improved accuracy.
