## D-003 — Relevance precision below the sensitivity threshold is not a decision
Date: 2026-08-12
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Treat the trio relevance band 0.15-0.35 as a single decision, not a precision exercise - the board outcome is identical across it.
Context: 0.75 was drafted from mention density, then corrected to 0.30. Testing the whole band showed 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 all produce the same board; only 0.5 and 0.75 differ.
