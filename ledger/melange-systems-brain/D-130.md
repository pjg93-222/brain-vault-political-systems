## D-130 — Mentat LDA quarter counts only after its LD-2 deadline
Date: 2026-10-09
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: Mentat's Giving Forecast anchors count an LDA quarter only once it has a filing AND its LD-2 deadline (Q1 Apr 20, Q2 Jul 20, Q3 Oct 20, Q4 Jan 20 of the next year) is on or before Melange's own sweep date (fullscope_receipts.json scope_sweeps.<final year>.sweep_through_dt_posted); the rule sits in lda.quarter_table, so it governs the partial year's through_quarter, the lobbying nowcast cut and the pooled blind errors; a missing sweep date leaves the LDA block unavailable, never the filings-only rule.
Context: Mentat lda.py sector_levels took through_quarter = the max quarter with filings > 0, and giving_forecast.py uses partial.through_quarter to pick the lobbying NOWCAST cut and its blind-error band (owner rulings 2026-10-08).
Rationale: One early filing broke the old filings>0 rule: crypto's single $0 Q3 2026 filing made it 'through Q3' with the nowcast cut at Q3 and Q3 observed as $0. The rule mirrors the Melange industry page (make_lobby_network_html.py QUARTER_DEADLINES, changed 2026-10-09). With the 2026-08-01 sweep only crypto changes (Q3 -> Q2); no forecast industry's through_quarter, nowcast or blind-error bands change.
