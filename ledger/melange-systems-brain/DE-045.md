## DE-045 — Validating a data migration with a chain run that starts at step 11
Date: 2026-09-19
Approach: Judging the chain healthy after a social_media.db migration (the N12 v2 interview truncation) from `update_bill_repository.py --rebuild-dashboard` / `run_pipeline.py --from verify_collector_roster_updates.py` plus the kernel suite.
Failure mode: that route never executes chain steps 1-10. The intake gate `audit_collection.py` (step 3) rejected the corpus three hours later, on the first FULL chain run: truncation had made four interview rows byte-identical to siblings ("4 exact duplicate interview-turn groups"), so every full chain, the daily wrapper and the weekly refresh halt at step 3.
Root cause: The refresh unit is deliberately a partial chain, the kernel tests use fixtures rather than the live DB, and nothing else runs the intake gate.
Would reconsider if: Never for a change to collected corpora. After any migration of social_media.db run the chain's own intake gate (`python fec\audit_collection.py --output <scratch path>`, about 10 minutes) and require ACCEPTED before calling the chain safe.
