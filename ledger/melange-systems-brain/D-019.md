## D-019 — OVERLAP is the staffer edge rule
Date: 2026-09-06
Status: ACTIVE
Decision: A person-person edge exists only for concurrent service in the same container: union intervals before intersection; an uncertain Senate payment window proves overlap only when fully covered by a certain interval; two uncertain windows never do. Non-overlapping same-office pairs get no edge. Sequence is a property of a person's trajectory (ordered stints, derived office→office moves), never a person-person tie. Every build reports the count of same-office pairs rejected for zero overlap; zero means the test is not firing. Inferred alumni ties live only in a separately labelled exploratory tier.
Context: Paul, 2026-09-03, "REMEMBER, OVERLAP". v4/v5 receipts: 12,498,349 guaranteed co-tenure edges; 16,001,459 zero-overlap pairs rejected of 31,139,699 candidates.
Rationale: Emitting edges across an office's whole history turns every office into a clique and fabricates relationships.
Reversal condition: None foreseen; an inference tier may be added beside observed edges, never merged into them.
