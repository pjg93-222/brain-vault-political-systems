## Audio speaker verification and ES cross-modal validation (2026-07-09; source: moneyball-audio-narratives-2026-07-09)

- **Audio speaker-verification (fixed the search-fetched contamination Paul flagged).** Search-fetched floor-speech audio was about 38% contaminated (multi-speaker panels, wrong-speaker, name collisions e.g. "Adam Smith" -> Adam Schiff). Fix per Paul's instruction "get transcripts from the audio to ID who is speaking":
  - `fec/b5_audio_verify.py` - per clip: yt-dlp auto-captions (cached in `b5_caption_cache.json`) + **Haiku 4.5** two-field classify: (1) speaker structure SOLO / MULTI / NOT_SPEAKING, (2) identity vs a per-member **identity card** (title/state/district/party/committees from `member_desk_bundle.json`). Identity judged from a **HARD signal only** (self-naming, "the gentlewoman from X", chair recognition, "my bill the __ Act") - **topic is nuance, NEVER a MATCH/MISMATCH** (Paul's key correction; a border-topic speech doesn't prove a border-state member). NONE signal -> NEUTRAL. keep = SOLO & identity != MISMATCH. Stores the evidence phrase per clip. Writes `b5_audio_verify.json` {clip:{speaker,identity,evidence,keep}}.
  - Head-to-head validated Haiku >= Fable on this task (Fable over-rejected clean solo speeches); **escalate to Sonnet, never Fable, if Haiku ever regresses** (see `fable-paragraphs-only`).
  - `fec/b5_audio_build.py` gained a verification gate: featurizes/aggregates only keep=true clips. Result: **395/445 members with >=180 s verified-clean audio**, 142 wrong-member clips caught, in `b5_audio_cache.json` (88 eGeMAPS v02, median about 17 min/member). Audio feeds ONLY validation scripts (`b5_audio_eval` / `b5_c_valid` / `b5_audio_ctcm` / `b5_audio_faithful`), never the engine.
- **ES cross-modal validation - RESOLVED = VALIDATED on clean audio (2026-07-09; final authoritative result at the time, then caveated).** The story took three turns:
  1. The contaminated hand-signed voice<->text +0.22 was provisional.
  2. On clean audio our COMMITTED acoustic-ES composite (`b5_audio_ctcm.py` / `b5_audio_eval.py`) gave -0.165 - but the es-voice-recheck workflow (32 agents, 21/24 findings survived) proved that composite is **MIS-SIGNED** (folk "steady/clean voice = calm"; its features actually track ES the OTHER way in clean data: F0stddevNorm +.14, jitter +.15, shimmer +.15, HNR -.18, all p<.01). So -0.165 was **real signal read upside-down, NOT a null**.
  3. The clean, PRE-REGISTERED, sign-choice-free test (`big5_es_crossmodal_prereg.md` -> `fec/b5_es_crossmodal.py`: ridge nested-CV, ALL 88 clean eGeMAPS -> big5_v2-ES, OUT-OF-FOLD, 5000-perm null, discriminant vs A) **PASSES: held-out r=+0.208, perm-p=.0002, ES-specific (r_A=-0.030)**. Held-out CV cannot be sign-hacked (the model learns the direction), which is why it was authoritative over the mis-signed hand composite. Caveats (honest): modest effect (about 4% var = the literature's true N-from-voice ceiling), voice<->text-MODEL convergence not ground-truth, minor C-overlap (F0 shares variance with C). At that time: ALL FIVE traits validated (A/C/E/O behaviorally + ES cross-modally). The committed composites in `b5_audio_ctcm` / `eval` are mis-signed and **SUPERSEDED by `b5_es_crossmodal.py`** - do NOT trust those two composites' signs. **DO NOT claim "ES falsified"** - that was the mis-signed composite, now corrected. (Note: the claim flip-flopped 3x while chasing evidence.)
  - **SUPERSEDING UPDATE (psych-audit session, same day, LOCKED robustness look @34bf7e4): the +.208 is GENDER-CONFOUNDED** - partial r(|gender,party) = -0.029 perm-p .715; within-gender null BOTH sexes (M -.016 n=282 / F -.036 n=113); the voice model was mostly reading speaker sex (F0).
