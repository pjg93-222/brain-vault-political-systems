## Three network configurations, one dial (2026-08-18; `holistic-network-configs`)

- `NET_CONFIG` = `cu` (default) | `holistic` | `holistic_norm`, fail-closed, config-keyed memo caches in `fec/moneyball_core.py`. The dashboard carries a matching three-way toggle (`data-net` = cu / holistic / norm) that reconfigures the whole page live: `setNetConfig()` rebuilds ADJ/EDGES/ELAYS then re-runs `buildPEBASE()` + `configureIssueNetwork()`, so PageRank/Katz/degrees genuinely recompute, not just the canvas.
- **CU REMAINS THE PUBLISHED NETWORK.** Both all-bills views carry a banner saying so. Holistic and normalized are exploratory surfaces; nothing published should be read off them. Their cosponsorship is all-bills but the press, committee-affinity and social layers stay CU-scoped, and cross-party damping still uses the CU-defined bipartisan prior, so the all-bills views are NOT a clean swap.
- **THE NORTON DIAGNOSTIC (the instrument that justified normalizing):** Eleanor Norton is a NON-VOTING DC delegate. Raw all-bills PageRank ranks her **#1 of 533**; normalized **#197**; CU #78. Raw all-bills top-3 = Norton / Fitzpatrick / Jayapal; normalized = Booker / Blumenthal / Higgins. A member who cannot vote on final passage leading an influence ranking is prima facie wrong, and that single case is a faster tell than any aggregate statistic. Keep using it as the check.
- **MEASURED AND REJECTED (do not re-propose without new evidence):** (a) dropping regular cosponsorship: it is only 12.3% of weight; removing it does not fix the artifact. (b) Per-edge volume normalization: association-strength `w/sqrt(Out*In)` left Spearman(Katz, raw tie volume) at **+0.552**, row-stochastic at **+0.477** (CU baseline +0.247, raw holistic +0.845). **You cannot remove a degree artifact from a metric that SUMS over edges**; per-edge scaling never reaches the sum. The per-member *mass cap* does, which is why it works and these did not.
- **ADOPTED:** chamber normalization (Senate/Senate ties are 7.5x heavier than House/House purely from pool size) + per-member total tie mass capped at **1.5x the median**, rescaled to the CU anchor 0.1333. `holistic_norm` loads DIRECTED and unsymmetrized by design: w(u to v) != w(v to u) carries real information. Built by `fec/network/build_holistic_normalized.py`, whose docstring records the full measurement trail.
- **Lessons that generalize (each cost a real error in this lane):**
  1. **A deployed user-facing number must be reproducible on the surface it annotates.** The normalized banner shipped claiming "#15 there, #149 here", a pair matching NOTHING: not the build docstring (Katz #1 to #37), not the live surface (PageRank #1 to #197), not an independent recomputation (#73 to #362). It survived from a superseded measurement. Corrected @d57aaa5df to the on-surface, named, metric-labeled figure.
  2. **Check conditioning before believing a normalization "improvement."** One candidate "passed" at +0.011 while spectral radius was 0.048, max/median 3e27, and the top-8 was alphabetically clustered: the metric had collapsed, not improved. rho and max/median are the honest instruments.
  3. **Read a producer's output dial from its source before running it.** Three unintended writes to live artifacts in this lane, all from GUESSING env-var names (`AUG_OUT` unset, a `KB_OUT` that does not exist, `DASH_HTML_OUT` instead of `DASHBOARD_HTML_OUT`; that one overwrote the live dashboard). Grep the dial out of the script first, every time.
  4. **A stale `.git/index.lock` was my OWN hung git processes**, not a foreign holder. Enumerate `Win32_Process` and diagnose before ever reaching for force.
- **PER-CONFIG NODE LAYOUTS @b9ff46982** (Paul: "Option 1, with the animated transition", reversing the earlier fixed-position choice). Each config now carries its own force layout and the field animates 600 ms between them. **THE HARD PART WAS THE COORDINATE FRAME, NOT THE ANIMATION** (generalizes to any visual comparison of two computed embeddings). A force layout is rotation/reflection invariant and `spring_layout` ends with `rescale_layout()` (normalizes every layout to max|coord|=1 about its OWN centroid), so orientation and absolute size are pure gauge. Rendering three independently computed, independently min/max-scaled layouts would animate that gauge as if it were structure: measured, naive = median shift **224** canvas units with corr(CU) 0.45/0.39, vs shipped = **75** with 0.58/0.69. **Two thirds of the naive movement was frame artifact.** Fix = (1) warm-start each layout from the published CU canvas so the solver lands in the NEAREST minimum, (2) Procrustes-align back onto CU (rotation + reflection + uniform scale + translation; reflection ALLOWED, no handedness to protect), (3) NO per-layout rescaler. Plus `k_cfg = 0.45*(mean_w_cfg/mean_w_CU)^(1/3)`, from the FR energy's invariance under (A,k) to (cA, c^(1/3) k), so unrelated weight units cannot masquerade as layout change. Committee layout is EXEMPT (its coords come from workbook membership; anchor labels are drawn at fixed points). Side effect: **repaired a latent bug**: area layout rebuilt PE on config change but never respaced, so lines were weighted by the new kernel over the old embedding while the caption claimed otherwise; median move 0 to 374. Party structure is STRONGER in the new layouts (1-NN same-party .936 holistic / .908 norm vs .783 CU), so the movement is signal.
- **COPY DISCIPLINE (the recurring one):** the fixed-position rationale was asserted in FIVE places, two user-visible (the `#netseg` tooltip, and `HOL_META['note']` which ships INSIDE `dashboard_data.json`). Reversing the behaviour without rewriting all of them would have shipped a false statement (same defect class as the banner). **When you reverse a design choice, grep for every place that explains it.**
