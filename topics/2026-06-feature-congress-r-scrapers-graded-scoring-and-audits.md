> Graded press scoring of 2026-06-14 to 06-21: the graded scale, lean-aware hidden coalitions, the audit passes on tail compression, crypto orientation, matching breadth, big-bank and anti-CU detection, the date-parse bug and the graded member by category sheet. ## Graded scoring, coalitions and audits (2026-06-14 to 2026-06-21, `cu_press_release_scraper.R` Stage 5)

### Graded scale (2026-06-14, augmented from four methods papers)
The CU press scorer (Stage 5) was augmented 2026-06-14 from four methods papers into a graded, multi-dimensional, coalition-aware scorer. The legacy integer `score` (-1/0/+1, NEUTRAL_BAND=2) is preserved bit-for-bit; new fields are ADDITIVE.
- **Graded scale (Haselmayer & Jenny 2017 + Rice & Zorn 2018):** `TONE_LEXICON` data frame carries per-stem intensity weights (1 weak / 2 mid / 3 strong) mirroring GEN_PRO_RX/GEN_ANTI_RX stems. `weighted_tone()` scans CU sentences applying valence shifters; predecessor tokens are derived from the prefix string (not a separate tokenization) so leading/double spaces cannot misalign them. NEGATORS_RX drops the word if it sits in EITHER of the two preceding tokens (2-token reach catches refusal frames "refuses to support"/"failed to protect" that a 1-token window scored as praise); AMPLIFIERS_RX x1.25 (`AMPLIFY_FACTOR`) and DOWNTONERS_RX x0.75 act on the immediate token only. Specific phrases + bill stances enter at weight 3. `graded_from_signed()` adds `+ 0` to collapse IEEE -0.0 -> +0.0. `graded_from_signed(Wp-Wn)` buckets |signed| by `GRADED_CUTS=c(1,2,3.5,5.5,8)` into 0/.2/.4/.6/.8/1.0 (rounded 1 dp).
- New scored columns: `score_graded` (+1..-1 by 0.2), `score_label` (symmetric "Strongly negative" etc.), `negativity_label` (user's framing: non-negative -> "Not negative (neutral or positive)", else 5 negative levels), `polarity` (Rice & Zorn normalized (Wp-Wn)/(Wp+Wn)), `pos_weight`/`neg_weight`.
- **Strong-tier anchor cap (`GRADED_STRONG_CAP=0.6`, added in the 2026-06-14 audit):** reaching "strongly"/"very strongly" (|graded|>=0.8) now REQUIRES a concrete CU anchor - a tracked-bill stance (`any(bills$signal != 0)`) OR an explicit PRO_CU_PHRASES/ANTI_CU_PHRASES hit. Tone-only releases (generic praise/criticism, no bill, no CU phrase) are capped at +/-0.6, with a "graded capped..." note appended to `evidence`. Sign and the legacy integer `score` are NEVER changed. Motivation: audit found 86 releases >= +0.8 vs only 2 <= -0.8, with inflated tops like Cotton +1.0 from the single word "applauds" and Barr +0.8 on a community-BANK release. Post-cap live: 28 demoted to +0.6; ALL 58 remaining +0.8/+1.0 are bill- or phrase-anchored. The phrase-hit `vapply` calls are hoisted (`pro_phrase_hits`/`anti_phrase_hits`) so they feed BOTH Wp/Wn and the anchor test.

### Hidden coalitions - LEAN-AWARE (reworked in the 2026-06-14 audit)
`detect_coalitions(cat_long, summary)` (computers-12-00037 clustering applied to legislators) builds a member x category mean-graded matrix. The original 1 - Pearson-correlation distance was scale/location-INVARIANT, so a strongly-hostile member shared "profile shape" with mild members and got absorbed into a bloc labeled pro-credit-union - the LIVE DURBIN BUG (Durbin, net -7, landed in "pro"). FIX: make each member's AGGREGATE LEAN (mean graded over all their signals, `tapply(cl$score_graded, cl$bioguide, mean)`) a heavily-weighted feature (`COALITION_LEAN_WEIGHT=3.0`) and cluster on EUCLIDEAN distance (sign+magnitude-aware) + Ward.D2; standardize dimension columns (`scale`) first so one high-volume issue cannot dominate. Drop zero-variance COLUMNS only - KEEP constant-lean rows (a uniformly hostile member is exactly the signal; Euclidean is defined on constant rows where Pearson was not - this is why the old row-drop is GONE). `members` output gains `overall_lean` (member's own mean graded); `profile` gains `mean_lean`; bloc lean is the mean of members' aggregate leans (not of per-issue means). Live: 7 blocs/90 members, a hostile-to-credit-union bloc now SURFACES (Durbin/Welch/Gooden/Lofgren/Warren/Wyden), Durbin correctly hostile (was "pro"). Sheets: "Coalition Members", "Coalition Profiles". `COALITION_MIN_MEMBERS=6`, `COALITION_MIN_SIGNAL=2`.
- **Unaligned separation (`COALITION_MIN_LEAN=0.05`, added in audit-pass-2):** cross-referencing bloc membership found the hostile bloc had 14 members but only 7 were real hawks - the other 7 had `overall_lean`==0 (all-neutral coverage, or pro/anti signals that cancel) and were force-assigned by Ward, dragging the bloc mean from ~-0.30 to -0.14 and mislabeling neutrals as "hostile". FIX: a coalition requires a STANCE - members with |overall_lean| < `COALITION_MIN_LEAN` are pulled out BEFORE clustering and reported as coalition 0 / "Unaligned (no clear lean)" (sorted to the bottom; constant-NEGATIVE hawks are still kept, only net-zero members are pulled). The aligned-only matrix is re-checked for newly-constant columns. Result: clean 6 stance blocs + 1 unaligned group; hostile bloc = exactly the 7 hawks, mean_lean -0.29. The `stage_outputs` console message now reports "N stance blocs ... ; M unaligned" (excludes coalition 0 from the bloc count).

### KNOWN/ACCEPTED - negative-tail compression (audit-pass-2, NOT a bug)
No release reaches -1.0 and only 2 reach -0.8. Root cause is structural and largely REAL: positive signals STACK (bills+phrases+praise -> Wp>=8 -> +1.0) but opposition does NOT - realistic max is opposing 2 CU-relevant bills at once (CLARITY+GENIUS = Wn 6; need >=8 for -1.0, >=5.5 for -0.8). Anti-CU TONE essentially never fires (0 releases hit an ANTI_CU_PHRASE): interchange hawks (Durbin/Welch/Lofgren/Gooden supporting the Credit Card Competition Act) frame attacks in POSITIVE language ("competition","fairness","small business"), so each scores only -0.4 from the bill stance. Hostility here is a MEMBER-level property (Durbin: 11 negative releases, net -7), not per-article intensity - captured by Member Summary `net_score` and coalition `overall_lean`, NOT by article `score_graded`. Minor noise: a few tone-driven graded-negatives (Warren/Duckworth -0.6) are pro-CONSUMER releases attacking banks/scammers; the legacy score correctly reads them 0. Did NOT recalibrate `GRADED_CUTS` (would desync the symmetric positive side and the papers' symmetric intensity).
- **IMPLEMENTED (2026-06-15): member-level stance rollup** in the Member Summary sheet - `member_lean` (avg `score_graded` over the member's releases, each once, -1..1), `member_stance` (symmetric `score_label`), `member_negativity` (the 6-level negativity taxonomy). Helper `member_graded_from_lean()` snaps the continuous mean onto the 0.2 grid (round-half-UP via +1e-9, clamp [-1,1]) so the per-article label functions work on it. CAVEAT confirmed live: the overall member label is diluted by cross-pressure - Durbin reads only "Very weakly negative" (member_lean -0.21) because he is pro-GENIUS (CUs like it) but anti-interchange; small-n members swing (Tammy Duckworth -0.60 "Negative" from 1 anti-bank-tone release). So `net_score` (Durbin -7) and the graded Member x Category sheet (Durbin Interchange -3.2) remain the better hostility-by-intensity views; `member_negativity` is the overall-lean summary, not a hawk ranking. NOTE: `member_lean` (per-release avg) differs slightly from coalition `overall_lean` (per category-row avg) by design.

### Anti-big-bank == pro-CU (audit-pass-5, 2026-06-15)
The user agreed to EXPAND relevance to capture bank-bashing AS pro-CU, on condition it is sorted correctly (not diluting the anti-CU signal). `detect_anti_big_bank(sents)` fires on a BIG-bank target (`BIG_BANK_TARGET_RX`: big/biggest/large/largest/mega/wall street/too-big-to-fail...) next to hostile framing (`BANK_BASH_RX`: exploit/gouge/predatory/greed/junk fee/bailout...) in a sentence that does NOT name a credit union (CU-naming sentences -> normal CU logic). In `score_release`: a release with only this signal becomes relevant="anti-big-bank (pro-CU)", scores pro (+2 to pro and +2 to Wp -> +1 / +0.4), and is tagged "CU-Bank Competitive Dynamics". GUARD: `anti_bank_pro <- anti_bank && (is.null(bills) || all(bills$signal >= 0))` so a tracked anti-CU bill (CCCA) still LEADS and bank-bashing never flips the sign positive. NOT a strong-tier anchor (caps at 0.6). Live: scored 510 -> 576 (+66), ALL on the positive side, NEGATIVES UNCHANGED at 29 (the user's no-dilution condition met); all 66 tagged CU-Bank Competitive Dynamics, flagged by the new relevance value for auditing.

### Anti-CU detection strengthened (audit-pass-6, 2026-06-15)

EVERY pattern stays CU-anchored AND keys on an inherently-hostile noun/claim, so a defensive "protect/preserve the credit union tax exemption" does NOT trip them (tested: PRO guard). Also broadened repeal verbs (+revoke/rescind) and the tax-noun list (+subsidy/giveaway/carve-out). `anti_phrase_hits` is already a strong-tier anchor, so a real attack can reach -0.8/-1.0. Live re-score: legacy dist UNCHANGED (29 neg / 182 neutral / 365 pos), 0 ANTI_CU_PHRASE hits in MEMBER press - confirming anti-CU attacks are genuinely absent from members' own releases (they attack via bills, not by name); the new patterns are precision-preserving (no pro flipped) and will activate on COMMITTEE tax/finance content. Complementary to the committee expansion.

### BUG FIXED - `extract_release` date parse aborted ~2107 fetches (audit-pass-7, 2026-06-15)
The "Full text retrieved for 10072 of 12219" gap was NOT random - failures concentrated in the `pagenum` scheme (2107/4521 = 47%; 15 SENATORS at 100%, incl. Senate Banking members Van Hollen/Reed/Collins/Ernst/Cruz...). Root cause: `extract_release` did `suppressWarnings(as.Date(substr(html_attr(t1,"datetime"),1,10)))` - but `as.Date()` THROWS (does not warn) on a non-ISO string, and the IQ-CMS senate.gov sites put a non-ISO value in `<time datetime>`. `suppressWarnings` does not catch errors -> `extract_release` aborted for the WHOLE page -> empty body -> counted as failed fetch. FIX: `attr_date()` helper wraps `as.Date` in tryCatch then falls back to `parse_release_date`; used for both `<time datetime>` and the meta-date tags. Validated live on ernst.senate.gov: now returns date 2026-06-05 + 2688-char body (was NA). SELF-HEALING: `fetch_member_fulltext`'s `done` set requires body AND date, so these empty-body releases are already in `todo` and auto-refetch on the next live run (no force needed). NOTE: these IQ-CMS pages lack article/main/.entry-content selectors so body falls back to whole-page `<body>` (incl. nav chrome) - works for scoring but a dedicated IQ-CMS body selector would be cleaner (follow-up). Also: 458 members at Stage 4 is NOT a bug - it is the topical funnel (537 roster -> 532 with indexed releases -> 458 with >=1 finance/CU-titled release); the 74 dropped are mostly local non-CU press, though a few finance senators (Crapo/Cornyn/Grassley) have poor index extraction yielding only nav labels.
