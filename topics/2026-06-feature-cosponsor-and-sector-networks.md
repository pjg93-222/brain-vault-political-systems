## Phase 1 - legislative-success edge weights (2026-06-25)

- Injection point: Stage 3 of `congress_member_scraper.R` (lines ~1882-1911), a DIRECTED sponsor-to-cosponsor graph keyed by bioguide; the old `Weight = Σ(1.0·original + 0.5·regular)` had no success/size/time weighting.
- `make_centrality_xlsx.R` reads the `Edges` sheet, igraph centrality, `InfluenceScore` = 0.60 WeightedOutDegree + 0.25 Betweenness + 0.15 Eigenvector, min-max to 0-100.
- Enhanced tie weight = `role × success × DecayWeight ÷ sizenorm(CosponsorCount)`.
- Success is effectively binary and chamber-aware: floor-passage of origin chamber vs stuck at introduced/committee. Bill chamber from Title prefix (H.R. = House, S. = Senate).
- `DecayWeight` already in data (0.8^age per congress). `sizenorm` options: off / 1/log2(1+C) / 1/√C / 1/C (Newman); the big "resolution" knob that de-noises blockbuster messaging bills out of WeightedOutDegree.
- Multipliers and thresholds are the user's to set; see `feedback_consult_multipliers_thresholds`.
- Deployed (x3, full network): enhanced Stage 3 (`role × success(x3 if passed-own-chamber) × DecayWeight ÷ √C`) gives `congress_cosponsor_network_20260625_184604.xlsx` (2390 edges), centrality, wired into the Blended Model `Centrality` sheet via Excel COM (0 errors, $2M, 494/533 coverage). Ranking effect: Hagerty #7 to #3, Tim Scott into the top 10 (Senate Banking bill-passers rewarded), HFSC core held. Scraper backed up to `congress_member_scraper.BACKUP-20260625.R`; model to `...PRE-CENTRALITY-20260625.xlsx`. NEXT then: stress-test this base, then Phase 2.
