## Primacy tier drift trace (2026-08-11, `primacy-tier-drift-2026-08-11`)

**Question (Paul, 2026-08-11): trace which factor moved Cortez Masto and Huizenga.** Kernel test `test_primacy_tier` fails on 2 ratified pins; drift confirmed pre-existing (see `ui-simplification-2026-08-11`). Full read-only trace + 2-agent audit (workflow wf_205ae2ff-25a); the mirror reproduced all 533 shipped `factor_ranked` values with **0 mismatches** and the live board exactly.

### The mechanism

- Board ranks on `factor_ranked` only - pillar pcts do NOT enter. Factor = sum(role_weight x act_weight) / sum(role_weight), + adjudication deltas, then shrunk toward an agenda-credit-weighted roster baseline (`shrink_anchor 15.0`, `uncertainty_penalty_k 0.25`; lambda=1 members untouched).
- Class weights: cu_primary 1.0, shared 0.5, bank/crypto/fintech 0.15, offtopic 0.0, letters 0.6, oppose -1.0. **An off-axis act does not score zero - it DILUTES** (full role weight into the denominator, 0.15 into the numerator).

### Root cause (proven by swapping only the tag universe, everything else fixed)

The 2026-08-10 association-anchor sweep, **633 -> 644 acts**. Pre-sweep board (blob at e1cb66e6c) vs live: Cortez Masto **T1 #10 -> T2 #19**, Huizenga **T2 #14 -> T1 #12** - an exact one-for-one Tier-1 swap, and the only two members crossing that line. Baseline moved 0.505089 -> 0.497095.

- **Cortez Masto falls** because she holds four newly-classified acts - S.1045/S.1790/S.4050 (executive-clawback, sponsor/original) + S.2669 (DAAMLA, cosponsor) - all bank/crypto at 0.15, none carrying an ACU position: +1.75 role-credit of denominator for +0.26 numerator. Removing just those four (others fixed) restores 0.5507 -> 0.5952, about rank 7.
- **Huizenga does not rise on merit** - his own factor barely moves; he is carried up as peers dilute and the baseline shifts. Blast radius is NOT local: the floors/universe move the baseline, so **511 of 533 members change rank** and 16 change tier, 9 of whom hold none of the acts.

- I first reported that the ACU SUPPORT flags on the five crypto acts were model-signed (`authority: L0:manual-adjudication`, `L2:quorum-issues`) rather than ACU declarations. **That was wrong.** `acu_position` traces to `source_data/legislation/acu_legislation_search_registry.json`, generated by `fec/legislation/build_legislation_search_registry.py` from the tracked, sha256-pinned `Moneyball_ACU_Search_Outline.docx` (hash verified byte-for-byte live).
