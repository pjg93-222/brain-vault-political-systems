### `pitch_variability_raw` (a SHIPPED contract metric) IS SEX-CONFOUNDED (reproduced independently 2026-08-20)

- On 1,324 clips / 309 members joined to `fec/legislators.csv` gender: **r(sex=F, pitch_variability_raw) = -0.266**, member-level **Cohen d = -0.944**, women 27.9% of clips but only 15.4% of the top quartile (51F/280M).
- **About 75% of the loading is the DENOMINATOR:** de-normalising (stddevNorm x amean) drops r to **-0.065**, while r(sex=F, F0 amean) = **+0.551**. Mechanism: `stddevNorm` divides a semitone (already log/ratio-scale) SD by the semitone MEAN, re-introducing pitch level - i.e. sex. `_stable_band` would stamp "low / flat, monotone delivery" on women at about 2x base rate. This is the exact failure mode that closed voice-ES permanently, and the artifact's own `explicitly_excluded` bars "absolute F0 or inferred gender as a personality input". **FIX: use plain semitone SD (stddevNorm x amean), not stddevNorm** (applied in 4193f0140; Paul-gated at the time of finding).
- **`topic_emotional_intensity` is sign-determined by construction:** the speaker baseline is the mean over ALL the member's units, so the z's sum to zero; with the contract floor of exactly 2 topics (`MIN_MEMBER_DISTINCT_TOPICS = 2`), `nA*mA + nB*mB = 0` EXACTLY - the two topic means are always opposite in sign. "This member gets more intense on topic X" is guaranteed true for one of any two topics regardless of the audio. Raising the floor does not fix it (one linear constraint on k means); it needs a within-member permutation null over topic labels (applied in 4193f0140).

### Gemini audio and agreement

- **Gemini audio IS routable - the skill's "NOT YET ROUTABLE" line is stale (re-tested 2026-08-19 on agy 1.1.11).** WAV still hard-refuses (`unsupported mime type audio/wav`), but **MP4 container works**: Gemini reads and transcribes the audio. Stage with ffmpeg (black 160x120 video + AAC audio). Two dispatch gotchas: (a) Gemini reaches for `ffprobe`, the headless shell denial fires, and the run **aborts with no report** - the contract must say read_file is the ONLY tool and that attempting a shell command destroys the report; (b) it emits `[00:06 - 00:08]` timestamps and inline `speaker male/female` tags, so any scorer MUST strip those before tokenizing or every score is deflated (v1 scored 0.609 mean where the true value was 0.945).
- **First transcription-AGREEMENT measurement this project has ever taken (2026-08-19, scratchpad-only; 30 fresh members + 3 silent controls, blinded, SEED=7, disjoint from the 51 consumed).** Gemini transcript vs cached YouTube caption for the same 60 s window: **mean bag-coverage 0.945, median 0.955, range 0.813-0.994; 25/30 at >=.90, 30/30 at >=.80**; longest contiguous matching run median 55 tokens, max 120. Excluding disfluencies and speaker tags: 0.953. **All 3 silent controls correctly answered NO with zero fabricated words** - the direct opposite of the 2026-07-26 result. Caveat that must travel with these numbers: **the reference is itself ASR (YouTube auto-captions), so this is recognizer-vs-recognizer AGREEMENT, not accuracy against human gold.** Residual disagreement is dominated by Gemini transcribing disfluencies the captions strip, name tokenization (`george h w bush` vs `hw`), and window-edge effects - inspected cases show Gemini often the MORE faithful of the two.
- **Transcription agreement is REFERENCE-DEPENDENT - quote the reference or the number is meaningless.** Same model, same task: **0.945** vs YouTube auto-captions (2026-08-19 run), **0.745** vs the whole C-SPAN program, **0.695** vs the C-SPAN target turn alone (disfluency-stripped, n=29). C-SPAN stenographic captions are ALL-CAPS and condensed; Gemini transcribes near-verbatim, so it agrees more with another ASR than with a human stenographer. Widening the reference from the target turn to the whole program only recovers about 5 points, so host speech is NOT the main cause.
